Privacy Policy

How Atlante Systems collects and uses personal information. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Atlante Systems is an IT consultancy and software studio. For privacy questions, contact atlantesystemsofficial@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, company name, billing details, support requests, and any information you send us by email or through a purchase form. If you buy through a payment provider/Merchant of Record, that provider may collect payment and transaction information under its own privacy policy.

3. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, provide quotes and services, process orders, deliver licenses/updates, provide support, prevent fraud/abuse, and meet legal or accounting obligations.

4. CRM data

Atlante Systems CRM is designed to store your business data locally and/or in your own Supabase project if you enable cloud sync. We do not receive your CRM customer records, attachments, or backups unless you intentionally send them to us for support.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary information with service providers who help us operate the business (for example payment processors, email providers, hosting, or accounting tools), only as needed for those services and subject to appropriate obligations.

6. Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, including legal, tax, and accounting requirements. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your personal information. Contact us at atlantesystemsofficial@gmail.com to make a request.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
