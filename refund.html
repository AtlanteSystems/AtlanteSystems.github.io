Refund Policy

Clear refund terms for Atlante Systems purchases. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

1. Software subscriptions and licenses

If you are not satisfied with Atlante Systems CRM, contact us within 14 days of your initial purchase and we will review your refund request. We may ask for your order details and the reason for the request so we can improve the product.

2. Subscriptions

You can cancel a recurring subscription before the next billing date to avoid future charges. Unless required by law or stated otherwise at checkout, subscription fees already paid cover the current billing period and are non-refundable after the 14-day initial purchase window.

3. Custom services

For consultancy, custom development, onboarding, or other bespoke services, refunds are handled according to the written proposal, statement of work, or invoice for that project. Work already delivered or time already spent may be non-refundable unless otherwise agreed.

4. How to request a refund

Email atlantesystemsofficial@gmail.com with your name, email used for purchase, order/invoice reference, and reason for the request. If your purchase was processed by a Merchant of Record such as Paddle, you may also be able to request assistance through their buyer support channels.

5. Exceptions

We reserve the right to refuse refunds in cases of abuse, fraud, excessive refund requests, or where the product/service has been substantially delivered as described. Nothing in this policy limits any statutory rights you may have under applicable consumer law.
